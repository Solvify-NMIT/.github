## 🚀 About Us

**Solvify Club** is one of the recently initiated clubs of the CSE department, NMIT. It is not only a club but a community that brings people from different mindsets together, with the motto to tackle real-world challenges. 

Prioritizing continuous learning, the club gives its members the opportunity to explore, learn, and experience the fields of both tech and real-world entities, fostering innovation and creative thinking.
